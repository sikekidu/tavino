Tavino Supplier Check

Before you wire the money, verify the supplier.

Send us a Chinese supplier — a company name or a 1688 shop link. Within 24–48 hours you get a PDF report that says who they really are, what the red flags are, and whether we would pay them. A remote desk check, done from the China side, in English.

What we check

  • Identity match — the shop name against the registered legal company behind it.
  • Official registry — registration status, capital, business scope, abnormal-operation and penalty records.
  • Factory or trader? — licence scope, self-description and product structure, cross-checked three ways.
  • Platform trade signals — shop age, transaction tier, repeat-buyer rate, any platform factory-inspection report.
  • Records — litigation and dishonesty listings (Full and Compare).
  • Price snapshot — your target product's dated tier prices and MOQ, positioned against two comparable suppliers (Full and Compare).

Every report ends with a verdict — Verified, Partially verified, or Could not verify — a red-flag list, and a one-line ordering recommendation. Every number carries its source and date. What we cannot verify, we say so; that is part of the answer.
