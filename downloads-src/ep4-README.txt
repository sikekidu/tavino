TAVINO — THE TWO-SIDED DOSSIER, EPISODE 4 — LIGHTING (HS 9405)
Built 26 Nov 2026. Desk check of public import records, not an on-site audit.

HEADLINE
The #1 "Chinese supplier" of US lighting is a Thai auto-trim factory. Of the eight
biggest names on the bill-count leaderboard, the two real factories are in Thailand
and Vietnam — and no identified Chinese household-lighting factory appears in the top
eight at all. What is left on the China side is a parcel shell, a US 3PL's Shenzhen
arm, a general trader and two unverified Guangdong names. In lighting, the
leaderboard measures offshore production and logistics, not Chinese manufacturing.

- ImportDoor (bill-of-lading records, window Jun 2018 – Oct 2026): HS 9405 total
  84,262 bills. All shares and rankings ONLY. China-origin bills 49,028 (58.2%).

  no product pages (404); bills filed before ~2022 under them are missing from the
  84,262 total. ImportDoor's total is a lower bound; ImportYeti's 1,516,727 is the
  only full-coverage figure.
- Ranking in this pack: aggregated across all 15 subheadings, filtered to mainland
